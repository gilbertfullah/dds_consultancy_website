The DDS Difference

Who We Are

A Sierra Leonean development research, policy advisory, and technology firm dedicated to evidence-based solutions.

We deliver locally grounded and technically rigorous solutions for governments, development partners, and civil society — ensuring that global standards are seamlessly integrated with local realities.

Our operating model is built on long-term partnership and hands-on implementation. We work alongside institutions to embed skills and build systems that outlast project cycles.
