What Are Cookies?

Small data files, significant impact on your experience.

Cookies are small pieces of text sent by your web browser by a website you visit. A cookie file is stored in your web browser and allows the website or a third-party to recognize you and make your next visit easier and more useful to you.

Types of Cookies We Use

We use different categories of cookies to balance functionality with privacy:

Category Purpose Lifespan
Essential Required for security and core site functionality. SESSION
Functionality Remembers your settings and language preferences. 1 YEAR
Analytics Helps us improve our research output and site speed. 2 YEARS

How to Control Cookies via Browsers

Beyond our settings, your browser provides deeper control over how cookies are handled.

Firefox

Menu > Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
